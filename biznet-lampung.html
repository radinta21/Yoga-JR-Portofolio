What I Do Day to Day

01

Branch & Asset Operations

  • Keep daily operations running smoothly across the branch and field teams.
  • Hold the office and warehouse to a standard of cleanliness, order, and security.
  • Make sure branch and warehouse assets are clean, maintained, and complete.
02

Inventory & Cash Control

  • Check material stock for installation, expansion, and maintenance through weekly and monthly stock opname, and watch for fraud.
  • Review petty cash use against its purpose and run weekly and monthly cash counts.
  • Organize the warehouse to SOP and distribute materials to each branch according to its needs.
03

Sales & Marketing

  • Monitor, forecast, plan, and execute sales activity and prospect closing against revenue targets, from yearly down to daily.
  • Plan marketing activity across both ATL and BTL channels.
04

People Management

  • Recruit, coach, and develop the team, with a daily briefing to set priorities.
  • Settle internal conflicts early so they do not affect service or targets.
05

Efficiency & Reporting

  • Track revenue efficiency and ways of working through a monitoring dashboard, in-out GA records, and Netop tools.
  • Report to Corporate Sales on a regular schedule.

06

Stakeholders & Expansion

  • Manage external conflicts and expansion into new areas.
  • Maintain working relationships with communities, B2B clients, and government offices.
